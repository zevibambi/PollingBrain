How PollingBrain works

PollingBrain's draft forecasts combine recent surveys, expert ratings and political fundamentals. The draft also models national, state and race-specific forecast error with Monte Carlo simulation. These inputs and assumptions need historical backtesting and verification before launch.

Detailed documentation, source links, and accuracy reports will be added as validation is completed.
